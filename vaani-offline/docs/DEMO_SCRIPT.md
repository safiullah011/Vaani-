# 2-minute demo script

1. **Hook (10 s):** "Notes in Hindi and English, with Wi-Fi off."
2. **Problem (15 s):** cloud tools need internet, cost money, and upload your private audio.
3. **Airplane mode (10 s):** turn Wi-Fi off on camera and show the header: *Cloud calls: 0*.
4. **Record (30 s):** speak a short Hinglish explanation of any topic.
5. **Result (30 s):** show the transcript, then the summary and action items.
6. **Ask my lecture (15 s):** ask one question, in Hindi or English.
7. **Proof (20 s):** open the CPU vs NPU tab and read your measured numbers.
8. **Close (10 s):** "Your voice, your language, your device."

Record it with the built-in Windows Snipping Tool or Xbox Game Bar (Win+G) and link the video in the README.
